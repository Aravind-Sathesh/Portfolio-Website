AskBITSians bridges the gap between current students and experienced professionals, creating a dedicated space for mentorship and knowledge sharing within the BITS Pilani community.

### Features
*   **Secure Access**:
    *   **Google OAuth Authentication:** Requires college email verification for exclusive access.
    *   **Privacy Controls:** Allows users to manage their visibility and contact preferences.
*   **Real-Time Communication**:
    *   **Instant Messaging:** A WebSocket-powered 1:1 chat system for direct communication.
    *   **Threaded Discussions:** Organizes conversations by topic tags for clarity.
*   **Community Forum**:
    *   **Q&A Platform:** A space to ask career or academic questions and receive expert answers from alumni.
    *   **Discussion Threads:** Enables meaningful conversations on various topics.

### User Experiences

#### For Students
*   **Career Guidance:** Connect with alumni at top companies like Google, Microsoft, and Goldman Sachs.
*   **Academic Support:** Get help with coursework and research projects.
*   **Interview Prep:** Practice interviews with experienced professionals in your target field.
*   **Resume Reviews:** Receive constructive feedback from hiring managers and recruiters.

#### For Alumni
*   **Give Back:** Share your journey and industry expertise with the next generation.
*   **Mentorship:** Guide and support students navigating their academic and professional paths.
*   **Network Building:** Connect with other alumni across different batches and industries.
